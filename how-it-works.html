How it works

Seven steps, one record.

The same event information is captured once and reused at every step — by you, the suppliers, the guests and, at the end, finance.

Clientsends the brief
Youshortlist, request, award
Suppliersquote, confirm
Guestsreply, travel on one itinerary
Financereconciles
1

Capture the brief

Dates, people, rooms, meeting space, flights, transfers, car hire, budget. One intake form for the client — or forward their email to your Smart Inbox and the brief is drafted for you to check.

BeforeRe-typing the client's email into a template, then chasing the two details it did not say.
With GroupBookThe brief is a record from minute one; the client sees a clean version and confirms it.
2

Shortlist from the directory

Search by town, capacity, layout and distance from the venue. Every listed hotel has its group-desk address on file, with the date it was last checked.

BeforePhoning switchboards to find who handles groups this month.
With GroupBookA shortlist in minutes, each property with a reachable group desk.
3

Send one request

One branded request to the whole shortlist, with a deadline and a reply address that files each answer against the brief. Any other supplier — a transfer company, an airline desk, car hire — can receive the same request.

BeforeOne email per hotel, copied and edited; replies scattered across an inbox.
With GroupBookSend once. Every reply lands on the brief, timestamped.
4

Compare and award

Quotes are read out of PDFs and emails into a side-by-side comparison — rates, totals, availability, conditions. A revised quote updates its own column. Pick the winner in one click; everyone else is declined automatically and hears back.

BeforeA spreadsheet built by hand from PDF quotes; hotels that never hear back, and stop answering.
With GroupBookRead for you, compared for you, everyone told; the decision and the quotes stay on the record.
5

Invite and look after the guests

One guest list. Invitations and reminders in your branding; guests reply online, give dietary and special requirements, choose sessions and sub-events; a QR code and check-in at the door for conferences. Each guest gets a personal itinerary on their phone — hotel, flight, transfer, car — that updates when anything changes. Online RSVP and guest page: in UAT with a launch customer

BeforeA spreadsheet of names, an RSVP supplier per event, confirmations typed one by one.
With GroupBookInvite, track and inform every guest from the same list.
6

Run the travel

From that same guest list. Rooms: room blocks per hotel, auto-assign, a locked rooming list sent to each hotel's group desk, the hotel's reference number carried back to the guest. Flights: legs and seat counts, bookings imported, passports captured for international routes, a passenger list per airline. Transfers: pick-ups and drop-offs, driver assignment, a transfer sheet per supplier, changes flagged until the supplier is told. Car hire: vehicle, drivers, pick-up and return. Supplier packs and vouchers come out of the record.

BeforeThree spreadsheets that disagree the night before arrival; the hotel has last week's list.
With GroupBookChange the list once; every rooming list, passenger list, transfer sheet and itinerary updates.
7

Settle

Import the card statement; match each line to a booking or purchase order; review only the exceptions; track purchase orders and budgets per event. Automatic matching: coming Q4 2026

BeforeTwo days a month in Excel. "Which Van der Merwe is this?"
With GroupBookStatement lines matched to the record; you check what doesn't fit.

The engine under all seven steps: the Smart Inbox

Forward any email or upload any PDF — a client's brief, a hotel's quote, a flight or hotel confirmation, a supplier invoice — and GroupBook reads it, works out what it is, and files it where it belongs: the brief, the comparison, the guest's itinerary, the reconciliation. New suppliers are recognised from what they send you. Anything it is unsure of is flagged for a person, not filed silently.

Guest portal and RSVP are in UAT with a launch customer. Automatic reconciliation matching is coming in Q4 2026; card-statement import and the reconciliation workspace run today.
