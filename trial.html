One sourcing job on us

Tell us if GroupBook fits your work, and run your next group brief through it free.

We are testing which plans, allowances and payment options suit group-booking teams, and we would rather ask the people who will use it than guess. If you complete the 12-minute plans-and-prices questionnaire and ask for it, we switch on one free sourcing job for your team (not the full Sourcing plan): a branded brief link to send your client, one round of requests to the hotels and venues you choose, every quote side by side, award and decline.

Start the questionnaire — about 12 minutesOnly have 60 seconds?

Offered to the first 20 eligible organisations that complete the questionnaire by 30 November 2026 and tick the box on its last screen to ask for the trial. We will set your workspace up within two working days. Your answers can be positive or negative: they do not affect whether you qualify.

What the trial includes

  • One sourcing job. One client brief, from your branded brief link or typed in, to one round of requests, to a side-by-side comparison, to award and decline.
  • Up to 10 suppliers. Hotels, venues or other suppliers from the directory, or ones you add yourself.
  • One named user for 30 days from the day we switch it on.
  • Set-up with a person. A 30-minute call to load your brief and send the first request together.
  • Your results. Everyone who answers gets what their peers said: how many group events they run, how big, and what they said the work was worth.

What it does not include

  • Guest lists, RSVPs, rooming lists, flights, transfers, itineraries or reconciliation — those are the Boutique and Full House plans.
  • Supplier contact details. The trial shows you which hotels and venues are in the directory and sends your request to them; the group-desk contacts themselves stay with the paid plans.
  • Hotel accommodation, guest management, or guaranteed quotes.
  • A second brief, a second round, or a second user. One trial per organisation.

The terms, in plain words

What to expect. Up to 10 requests. Hotels typically reply within a few days, and about 1 in 4 send a quote. If a request bounces, we correct the address and resend it, and it does not use up one of your 10. You are part of a founding group of 20: a real brief run through the real tool, not a guaranteed result. The directory is checked regularly, but we do not promise that every address or detail is correct.

Requests are real. Every request goes to a real hotel or venue under your organisation's name, so the brief must be a real one. You are responsible for what it says.

Fair use applies, the same as on every plan. A trial used to collect supplier contacts rather than to run a brief is ended.

No service levels. Support is by email with a reference number; we fix our defects at no charge, but the trial carries no response-time commitment.

After 30 days the workspace is read-only: you can still open and export your quotes, but nothing more is sent and the brief link closes. If you subscribe, everything carries over as it is.

Your data. Kept for 60 days after the trial ends, then deleted unless you subscribe. The GroupBook privacy policy and POPIA operator terms apply from day one. Either side can end the trial at any time by email.

No card, no debit order, nothing to cancel. No VAT is charged by PanDaan (Pty) Ltd.
