Pricing

Pay for the jobs you do. We are setting the numbers with the people who will use it.

One group event is four jobs: find the venue, invite the guests, run the travel, settle the bill. In South Africa they are often done by different people, so GroupBook is sold in four modules on one size scale. You take the modules you use; a specialist can take theirs on the same event. The prices are not on this page yet, on purpose: we are asking agencies, event teams and travel desks what each job is worth before we print a number.

No prices are shown in the survey either. Everyone who answers gets the results: what their peers run, how big, which jobs they keep in-house, and what they said each was worth. Monthly on a debit order, cancel at the end of any paid period, or annual at ten months for twelve; no VAT is charged.

Before you choose

When will the prices be published?

When the research closes. If you answer the survey, you hear first, and the people who help us set the prices are offered the best terms we will ever offer.

Do I have to take all four modules?

No. Take the jobs your team does. If a specialist does your RSVP or a travel desk does your rooming lists, they can take their module and work on the same event; nobody pays twice for one event and nobody pays for a job someone else does.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly plans run on a debit order and can be cancelled at the end of any paid period. Annual plans are invoiced once at ten months' price for twelve months' use.

Is there a set-up or implementation fee?

No. Remote onboarding — workspace set-up and one 60-minute session with your administrator — is included with any module.

What counts as a user?

A named login. Each size includes its users and has a maximum — Small 2 (up to 5), Mid 5 (up to 10), Large 10 (up to 20); further named users can be added up to the maximum, and the size applies to every module you take.

What is a guest event, and when is it charged?

Running the RSVP for one event in GroupBook — invitations, online replies, the guest page, check-in — sized by the guests who registered. Events up to your size's guests per event are included; more guests than that are charged per extra guest, never more than a set price for that size of event, shown and approved before anything is charged. A guest list imported for rooms, flights or transfers is never charged.

Do hotels pay to be listed or to reply?

No. Hotels and venues are listed and receive requests at no charge. The only thing we ask of them is a current group-desk contact.

Why is there no VAT on the price?

PanDaan (Pty) Ltd is not a registered VAT vendor, so no VAT is charged on any GroupBook invoice. The price shown is the amount invoiced.

What the roadmap lines mean

Coming Q4 2026 is being built now. Nothing on this page with that tag is charged for until it ships.

By agreed scope means a capability for larger organisations, delivered under the order form, not switched on off the shelf. Anything without a tag runs in production today.
